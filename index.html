What the camera sees
RGB

Each line is how much that colour in the measured patch changes from moment to moment (slow drift removed). A heartbeat shows up as a steady, repeating wobble, strongest in green.

Accuracy log0 readings

Each reading is saved with the reference you typed. The page shows the average error against it.

Not a medical device. Keep still and well lit. Contact mode: the rear camera and flash touch your skin. Ear and wrist signals are weaker than the finger, so they may need a few tries.
